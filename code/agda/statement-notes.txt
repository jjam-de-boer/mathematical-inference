Statement ledger

Notes for identities whose Agda statement is a pointwise product or a
telescope equality. These notes are not part of the checked development.

- union-complement holds at every sample:
  union E (complement E) x ≡ topEvent x.
- Finite coordinate listings and their round trip live in Thesis.Fin
  (finRange-nodup, occurrences-finRange, ofFn-get, ofFn-cong).
- Rational magnitude is Equiv, the cross-multiplication relation.
- Adding a witnessed difference back to the subtrahend is restore-sub.
- Dividing by a presentation equivalent to the unit is div-by-unit.
- FiniteProbRecord.map is pushforward. map_id and map_comp are equality of
  records. The denominator equation has a unique proof because natural-number
  identity is decided, and the positivity witness is unique by the order
  constructors.
- product_probVal_equiv_of_slices is product-probVal-slices: each common
  value has an Equiv of its two slices.
