Statement ledger

Notes for identities whose Agda statement is a pointwise product or a
telescope equality. These notes are not part of the checked development.

- union-complement holds at every sample:
  union E (complement E) x ≡ topEvent x.
- Finite coordinate listings and their round trip live in Thesis.Fin
  (finRange-nodup, occurrences-finRange, ofFn-get, ofFn-cong).
- Rational magnitude is Equiv, the cross-multiplication relation.
- Adding a witnessed difference back to the subtrahend is restore-sub.
- Dividing by a presentation equivalent to the unit is div-by-unit.
- FiniteProbRecord.map is pushforward. map_id and map_comp are equality of
  records. The denominator equation has a unique proof because natural-number
  identity is decided, and the positivity witness is unique by the order
  constructors.
- product_probVal_equiv_of_slices is product-probVal-slices: each common
  value has an Equiv of its two slices.
- membershipEvent_cons, unionList_map_singletonEvent, and
  membershipEvent_filter hold at each candidate. Identity of samples is
  an explicit decision procedure.
- perm_of_nodup_mem_iff is perm-of-same-members: each entry has a function
  from membership on the left to membership on the right and a function in
  the other direction. Occurrence counts use that same decision procedure.
- independent_iff_condVal_left, independent_iff_condVal_right,
  conditionalIndependent_iff_condProduct, and
  conditionalIndependent_iff_condVal are pairs of the two directions.
- assignmentDecidableEq is assign-≟. An assignment is a telescope, and
  assign-ext rebuilds it from agreement at each coordinate.
- rectangularEvent_eq_true_iff is the pair rect-holds. Enumeration is an
  ordinary list: the telescope is a nested pair in the universe of the
  coordinate types.
- rectangularEvent_assignment_congr is rect-agree. Coordinatewise agreement
  is telescope equality, and the rectangle is unchanged by that equality.
- FiniteProduct.atoms is jointAtoms. FiniteProduct.record is productRecord.
  Zero-weight atoms are kept.
- qProduct_singleton selects with pick, the Boolean of coordinate identity.
  The two families are not equated; the product is compared pointwise.
